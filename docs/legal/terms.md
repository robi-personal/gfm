## 9. Push notifications

Per-form push notifications are a premium feature. By enabling notifications on a form, you authorise us to:

- Register a Google Forms `watch` on that form.
- Receive Pub/Sub deliveries from Google when a new response is submitted.
- Send a push notification to your registered devices through Firebase Cloud Messaging.

Notifications contain the form's **title** and the fact that a new response was received — never the response content itself.

Google Forms watches expire automatically after 7 days. To continue receiving notifications, re-enable the toggle in the form settings.
