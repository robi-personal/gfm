Workflow

From zero to published form in under 45 seconds.

01

Sign in with Google

Your existing Google account. GFM requests only the permissions it needs — nothing more.

02

Create or open a form

Tap + to name a new form, or open any existing one. Forms live in your Google Drive.

03

Build, share, and review

Add questions, enable quiz mode, hit share. Responses appear in the app the moment they arrive.

Privacy-First

Your data never leaves Google.

We built GFM without a backend on purpose. There's no server to breach, no database to leak.

No Backend

GFM has no servers. Every API call goes directly from your phone to Google.

Minimal Permissions

GFM only sees Drive files it created. It cannot read your broader Drive.

No Content Logging

Form content, questions, and response data are never sent to analytics or crash services.

Questions about data handling? Read our Privacy Policy.
