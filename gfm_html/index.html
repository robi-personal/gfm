Workflow

From zero to published form in under 45 seconds.

01

Sign in with Google

Your existing Google account. GFM requests only the permissions it needs — Drive files it creates and form responses.

02

Create or open a form

Tap + to name a new form, pick a template, use the AI builder, or open an existing form from your Drive.

03

Build, share, and review

Add questions, enable quiz mode, hit share. Responses appear the moment they arrive — with a push notification if you want one.
